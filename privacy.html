01 / who we are

hello. we’re idio.

Idio Creative, based in Virginia, United States, is responsible for the information handled through this website. Contact us about privacy at idiocreative@outlook.com.

This notice describes our current development website, including its newsletter signup and browser storage. It does not replace any separate privacy terms provided for client projects or contracts. We do not currently run accounts, online checkout, advertising trackers, or website analytics.

02 / information & purposes

what comes with a visit.

  • Technical delivery and security: our hosting provider receives information needed to serve the site, including your IP address and request details. GitHub Pages states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security.
  • Browser choices: your storage preference and its dates. If you accept the shorter intro, that preference selects “make it yours”, followed by “make it idio”, on homepage visits. These records stay in your browser; our code does not send them to Idio.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address and opt-in are sent to Brevo when you submit the form. Brevo also receives connection information such as your IP address to process the request and protect its service.
  • Messages you send us: your email address, name if supplied, message, and any attachments or project details you choose to send. We use these to respond, discuss work, and manage the resulting relationship.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we use consent for the optional shorter intro preference and newsletter subscriptions. We use correspondence to respond to your request or take steps toward a contract. Service delivery, proportionate security, and necessary records may rely on legitimate interests or legal obligations where permitted. We do not use this site for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Do not send sensitive personal information or confidential credentials through newsletter signup or an initial inquiry.

03 / cookies & browser storage

a little memory.
a real choice.

Our website code does not set HTTP cookies. It uses localStorage, a different kind of browser storage. Privacy rules can apply to both, so our “cookie settings” control covers this storage too.

idio-privacy-v1Remembers acceptance or rejection, notice version, and choice dates. A valid acceptance selects the shorter homepage intro. Saved when you choose; not used for tracking.180 days. An expired or invalid record is removed when the site next checks it.

We do not install analytics, marketing pixels, third-party social embeds, or cross-site tracking on this website. Social buttons are links; the other website’s practices apply after you follow one.

04 / your choices

you’re in control.

Accept optional storage, reject it, or save a customized choice. Accept and reject are equally available. Rejecting does not prevent you from browsing or signing up for the newsletter. With a valid opt-in, homepage visits show “make it yours”, then “make it idio”. Without it, visits show the full intro.

The full intro is enabled unless a valid acceptance for the shorter intro was previously saved in this browser.

Open cookie settings →

Closing the first popup with × or Escape rejects optional storage. Closing settings after making a choice keeps the saved choice. You can withdraw acceptance at any time by reopening settings and choosing Reject optional. That restores the full intro for future homepage visits in this browser; other open tabs update when the browser delivers the change. An intro already playing finishes its current sequence.

We ask again after 180 days or if you clear your preference. Choices apply to this site origin, browser, and browser profile—not automatically to other devices. Private browsing may discard them. If saving fails, the shorter intro remains off for the current page and we explain how to continue.

Without JavaScript or a supported dialog: the shorter intro is not applied. Without JavaScript, the page opens without the animated loader. Use your browser’s site-data controls to remove existing Idio storage. Clearing site data also removes your saved rejection, so the popup can appear again. Contact us if you need help.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track: we do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or track visitors across websites, regardless of these signals. The signals do not subscribe you to email or turn on the shorter intro.

05 / newsletter & email

your inbox is yours.

Our optional newsletter sends Idio news and event emails through Brevo. The signup box is unchecked by default. Submitting with that box checked requests a subscription; accepting browser storage does not subscribe you.

If Brevo sends a confirmation email, follow its instructions to complete signup. We do not treat our on-page acknowledgment as proof of email ownership. Unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email, or contact idiocreative@outlook.com.

Brevo processes signup, delivery, security, and subscription records. Depending on campaign settings, emails may also use open or link measurement; website cookie settings do not control email measurement. You can unsubscribe from those emails or contact us about the current settings. See Brevo’s Privacy Policy.

Without JavaScript, the signup form submits directly to Brevo and may take you to its website. That provider’s own cookies, notices, and controls apply there; our website settings do not control another domain.

Unsubscribing stops marketing but does not necessarily delete your contact record. Request deletion separately if that is what you want. A limited suppression record may be needed to avoid sending further marketing.

06 / providers & transfers

who helps things run.

  • GitHub Pages: website hosting and security. Read GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • Brevo: newsletter forms, contact management, email delivery, and service security.
  • Email providers: when you write to our Outlook address, Microsoft and your email provider process the communication. Read Microsoft’s Privacy Statement.

These services may process information in the United States and other countries, where privacy laws can differ from those where you live. Any required transfer safeguards depend on the applicable law and provider arrangement; a cookie choice is not permission for every international transfer.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or where necessary to protect legal rights and service security. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

07 / retention & security

only for a purpose.

Browser-record durations are listed above. Newsletter details are held in Brevo to manage the subscription and related consent or suppression records. Inquiry and client correspondence may be retained for the relationship, necessary business records, and applicable legal obligations. Provider logs and backups follow the provider’s retention arrangements. Contact us for information about retention or to request deletion of a particular record.

The website uses HTTPS when served through GitHub Pages and restricts executable content and external connections. Newsletter requests made by our JavaScript omit cookies and referrers. No system can guarantee absolute security. Our team handles privacy requests and manages provider access and retention.

08 / your privacy rights

let’s put you first.

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have rights to information about processing, access, correction, deletion, a portable copy, withdrawal of consent, objection or restriction, and an appeal or complaint. Some rights have exceptions. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Send a request to idiocreative@outlook.com with the request and the address you used to contact or subscribe to us. You can also ask through an authorized representative where the law allows. We may need proportionate verification before releasing or deleting information. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested through an appropriate process.

We handle requests under applicable law and its deadlines, and do not deny ordinary website access because you exercise privacy rights. If you disagree with our response, reply asking for a review. You can also contact the privacy regulator or other competent authority in your jurisdiction, including your Canadian federal or provincial authority or Brazil’s ANPD where applicable.

09 / children’s privacy

If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can review it and take appropriate deletion or other protective action. Do not submit a child’s personal details through the newsletter form.
